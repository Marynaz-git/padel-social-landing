# Padel Social Vol. 2 — Design Brief

Design a complete responsive landing page for an event brand called PLAY PADEL CLUB.

The event is:

PADEL SOCIAL — Vol. 2
03 October 2026
W Padel Country Club, Lisbon

15:00–17:00 — Tournament
17:00–21:00 — After Padel / Drinks & DJ

The website should NOT look like a generic sports tournament website.

The main idea of the brand is:

PADEL × PEOPLE × SOCIAL LIFE

The experience should feel like:
come for the game → stay for the people, music, drinks and atmosphere.

--------------------------------------------------
VISUAL DIRECTION
--------------------------------------------------

The visual identity should combine:

1. Premium editorial / fashion aesthetic
2. Modern sports culture
3. Social club / event energy
4. Warm Lisbon sunset atmosphere

Think of a mix between:
- premium tennis/padel club
- fashion editorial
- Mediterranean social club
- modern event landing page

The design should feel aspirational, stylish and social — not corporate, not tech/SaaS, and not like a nightclub website.

The website should communicate:
sport during the day → golden hour → drinks, DJ and socializing.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT make every section white.

Create visual rhythm by alternating between:
- warm cream / ivory sections
- black / very dark charcoal sections
- photography-led sections
- occasional yellow-orange gradient accent sections

The page should feel dynamic while still belonging to one consistent design system.

--------------------------------------------------
COLOR SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------

Primary background:
warm ivory / cream — not pure white.

Primary text:
almost black / deep charcoal.

Dark sections:
rich black / charcoal.
Avoid flat SaaS-like #000000 blocks when possible; use a sophisticated warm black.

Brand accent:
warm orange → golden yellow.

The accent should feel closer to:
mango / amber / sunset orange / golden yellow.

IMPORTANT:
Avoid reddish-orange.
Avoid neon acid yellow.

The Play Padel logo uses an orange-to-yellow gradient, so the website should visually connect to this palette without using the gradient everywhere.

Use orange/gold mainly for:
- CTAs
- small labels
- section numbers
- dividers
- highlights
- selected words
- hover states

Do NOT make large parts of the interface orange.

--------------------------------------------------
TYPOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Typography is extremely important.

Use a strong contrast between:

EDITORIAL SERIF
for:
- major headlines
- oversized statements
- section titles
- emotional messaging

and

CLEAN MODERN SANS SERIF
for:
- navigation
- event information
- buttons
- captions
- schedules
- small labels

The serif should feel elegant and fashion-editorial, slightly “old money”, but the overall site must still feel contemporary.

Use typography as a graphic element.

Allow very large headlines.

Examples:

PLAY
PADEL
SOCIAL

MORE
THAN PADEL.

COME FOR THE GAME.
STAY FOR THE PEOPLE.

PLAY. MEET. STAY.

Do not overuse rounded cards.

Avoid the typical SaaS UI style.

Prefer:
editorial grids,
large typography,
photography,
thin lines,
spacing,
strong composition.

--------------------------------------------------
PHOTOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Photography should play a major role.

Visual atmosphere:
warm golden hour / early evening in Lisbon.

Show:
- people playing padel
- stylish men and women around 25–40
- people laughing and talking
- drinks
- DJ equipment
- rackets and balls
- padel courts
- sunset
- close-up lifestyle details
- groups socializing after matches

The visual balance should be approximately:

40% SPORT
60% LIFESTYLE / COMMUNITY / SOCIAL

The website should sell the feeling of being there, not only explain the tournament.

Avoid generic stock-photo aesthetics.

Images should feel candid, cinematic, warm and premium.

--------------------------------------------------
UI PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------

Avoid:
- excessive rounded cards
- generic gradients
- glassmorphism
- SaaS dashboards
- tech/startup styling
- overly playful sports graphics
- excessive icons
- shadows everywhere
- large blocks of body copy
- pure white everywhere
- reddish orange

Prefer:
- editorial grids
- elegant typography
- strong photography
- whitespace
- thin dividers
- oversized type
- asymmetrical compositions
- image crops
- subtle motion
- clean interactions

--------------------------------------------------
MOTION / INTERACTION IDEAS
--------------------------------------------------

Use subtle sophisticated interactions:

- slow image reveal on scroll
- editorial text appearing line by line
- gentle parallax on large photography
- horizontal image gallery
- subtle marquee:
  PADEL · PEOPLE · SOCIAL · MUSIC
- schedule numbers moving slightly during scroll
- elegant button hover transitions
- image zoom of approximately 2–3% on hover

Motion should feel premium, not flashy.
